Metronome tips

On iPad, tap Start once to unlock audio. Keep this tab active for best timing; iPadOS may suspend browser audio when you switch apps or lock the screen. Tap Tempo averages recent taps. Keyboard: Space = Tap Tempo; Enter = Start/Stop; ↑/↓ = ±1 BPM; Shift+↑/↓ = ±5 BPM; Esc = Stop. Shortcuts are ignored while editing fields.
